# Level Eligibility{id=placementeligibility}

A level must be **rated in-game** and must be considered *harder than the current demon placed at #150* in order to be placed on the Demon List. That being said, some unrated levels might result in being eligible for the Rated+ tab if the jury, outside advisers unrelated to Pointercrate, considers them worthy of being there.

The Rated+ list handles placements differently from the classic list. After a level is nominated by the list team and votes are cast by an independent committee, it is placed into a queue that sorts all candidates from highest to lowest approval rating. Levels that reach the top of the queue will be periodically placed on the list, a system meant to give all levels a fair chance while maintaining a reasonable workload for the people involved; please note that it will almost certainly change over time in service of that goal.

All levels must be legitimately verified by a single player, including solo verifications of 2-player levels. Levels will only be placed if proof of verification is provided to the list team, and this proof must meet our current standards for record eligibility.

Rated levels without verification proof can also be placed once completed by another player, regardless of verification rights.
