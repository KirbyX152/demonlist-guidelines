# Timing of Placements{id=timing-of-placements}

We will place a list-worthy level **one month** after it gets rated in-game. If a level is unquestionably the new #1 demon, it may be placed on the list **sooner or instantly**. Levels using the 2-player gamemode will be placed no later than three months after their solo verification, given at least one month has passed since the verification.

Please note that this guideline does **not** apply to levels with a hacked verification; the one-month timeline for a hacked level begins after it receives its first legitimate victor. In addition, this one-month timeline will **reset** if the level receives an [update](/guidelines/levelupdates/) with a legitimate verification.

## Rated+ Placements{id=rated-plus-placements}

After a level is nominated by the list team for the Rated+ Tab and votes are cast by an independent committee, it is placed into a queue that sorts all candidates from highest to lowest approval rating. Levels that reach the top of the queue will be periodically placed at a rate determined at the list team's discretion, and will be placed **one month** following their approval. This system is **subject to change** due to Rated+'s infancy.
